Why Pathé Pulse?

On weeknights, many auditoriums are half empty while running costs stay the same. For viewers, a night out at the cinema is still passive: you sit, you watch, and when the credits roll everyone leaves without a word.

Pathé Pulse fills these slots with social, participatory events, without touching what makes cinema special: the film itself.

How an evening works

Example: the night of Lucas, 21, a student in Grenoble.

  1. Before: he finds it and booksA TikTok teaser, then the €8 pack paid in two taps in the app with his Pass Culture.
  2. 7:00 PM: the welcomeThe Pop-Hub zone replaces the queue: app check-in, photo call, a student host, a themed drink.
  3. 7:30 PM: the quiz in the auditoriumEveryone plays on the giant screen with a live leaderboard. Vote with the app or as a team.
  4. 7:45 PM: Sanctuary ModeThe host announces it, phones go away. The film begins.
  5. After: the loungeChat about the film, vote for next week's theme, leave with a collector card.

Sanctuary Mode

During the screening: no phones, complete silence. All the fun happens before and after the film. Big-screen purists get their auditorium back, everyone else gets the atmosphere.

Designed for everyone

Our 12 interviews, from age 13 to 74, shaped the concept. Here is how it welcomes everyone.

Reasonable hours

Start at 7:00 PM, finish around 9:30 PM at the latest: works with school, work the next morning, and seniors' transport.

Everyone can join

Team voting or clickers provided on site, so you can play with or without the app.

Friendly words

We say "meetup": open, well hosted, and everyone can speak.
